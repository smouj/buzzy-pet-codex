# Provenance and release record

## Source and process

The character reference was supplied by the repository author as a small PNG of the Theme Park World advisor. The final poses were developed with AI-assisted image generation and deterministic extraction, fitting, chroma removal and edge cleanup. The reference was used to preserve the black body, tall white eyes, antennae and white gloves.

The distributed sheet is the approved existing nine-row version. It keeps the final cleaned artwork rather than inventing missing v2 look poses. Leftward motion is reflected frame by frame from the rightward row. Jumping preserves upward displacement and returns to the resting baseline.

## Public demo

`previews/preview-pet-buzzy.mp4` is a recording supplied by the author of the companion in use. It is a demonstration video, not an installation asset. The public copy has no audio track or user-authored container metadata; the repository also includes a still poster.
